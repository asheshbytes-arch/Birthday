a letter for you

Dear D,

Happy Birthday, Baby. ❤️

I don't know if I should write all this or not, but today is your birthday, and I couldn't just wish you normally and end it there. So I'm writing whatever was in my heart.

We haven't talked since 23rd September. In these days, there were so many times when I opened my phone, felt like texting you, and then stopped myself. Shayad kuch cheezein bina bole hi reh jaati hain.

You once said that there is affection between us, but not love. Maybe it will take me some time to accept that, because for me, what I felt was never just affection. I genuinely wanted to spend my whole life with you.

I never imagined our future as some perfect picture. It was always just you — random calls, silly little fights, annoying each other, then making up, talking for no reason at all. I thought maybe one day, all of this would simply become our normal life.

But maybe it wasn't written in our destiny. Maybe you were meant to become a very important part of my life, not necessarily stay in it forever. And you will always be that part of mine.

One thing I'll always remember — ab shayad tumhare phone mein Tumhari favourite notification kabhi nahi aayegi. All those Rho 2 wali walks, cooking together, and all those little moments we spent together... these memories mean so much to me.

I'm not writing this letter to make you feel guilty, to ask for anything back, or to change your decision. I'm writing it simply because your birthday matters to me. And you matter to me too.

I really hope all your dreams come true. Tum jahan bhi raho, jiske saath bhi raho, I hope you are always happy in your own little world. I hope you get everything you wish for, and ab tumko aisa banda mile who truly understands you and keeps you genuinely happy.

And if someday you remember me, I hope tumhare face par sadness se zyada smile aaye, remembering all those times jo humne saath mein spend kiye. Because whatever we had, it was real for me. Maybe humari kismat mein bas itna hi saath likha tha, but jitna bhi tha, mere liye bahut special tha. And I'll always be grateful for it.

Happy Birthday, D. Take care of yourself. 🤍

— A
